# ServisMasterOS — Pricing & Plans

> Version: 1.0 · Last updated: November 2026

## Plans Overview

| Plan | Price | Trial | Users | Orders/Month | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | €25/mo | 14 days | 1 | 50 | Email |
| **Pro** | €50/mo | 14 days | 5 | 300 | Email + Phone |
| **Enterprise** | €100/mo | 14 days | Unlimited | Unlimited | Priority + On-site |

---

## Basic — €25/mo

**For small shops just starting out.**

### Included:
- ✅ Vehicle intake (digital work orders)
- ✅ Up to 50 work orders per month
- ✅ 1 user account (owner)
- ✅ Inventory (up to 100 items)
- ✅ PDF work orders
- ✅ Client portal (live status)
- ✅ Email notifications
- ✅ SMS reminders (50 per month)
- ✅ Database backup (daily)

### Not included:
- ❌ Multiple users
- ❌ Telegram notifications
- ❌ WhatsApp integration
- ❌ Tax/Fiscalization integration
- ❌ API access

---

## Pro — €50/mo

**For shops with 2-5 mechanics.**

### Everything in Basic, plus:
- ✅ Up to 300 work orders per month
- ✅ 5 user accounts
- ✅ Unlimited inventory
- ✅ Telegram notifications
- ✅ WhatsApp integration
- ✅ Advanced analytics (KPIs, mechanic performance)
- ✅ Vehicle service history book
- ✅ SMS reminders (200 per month)
- ✅ 24h email support

---

## Enterprise — €100/mo

**For large shops and chains.**

### Everything in Pro, plus:
- ✅ Unlimited work orders
- ✅ Unlimited users
- ✅ Tax/Fiscalization integration (PFR)
- ✅ API access (REST + webhook)
- ✅ B2B portal for corporate fleets
- ✅ Custom reports
- ✅ On-site training
- ✅ Priority support (4h SLA)

## Billing

- **Monthly** — Credit card, Bank transfer
- **Yearly** — 2 months free (16.7% discount)
  - Basic: €250/year (instead of €300)
  - Pro: €500/year (instead of €600)
  - Enterprise: €1,000/year (instead of €1,200)

- Cancel at any time
