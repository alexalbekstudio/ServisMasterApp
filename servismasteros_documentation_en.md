# 🔧 ServisMasterOS

**Complete operating system and SaaS platform for modern auto repair shops, garages, and fleet maintenance.**

---

## ✨ What ServisMasterOS Does

### 🚗 Complete Workshop Digitalization in One Place

1. **📋 Digital Vehicle Intake & Photo Documentation** — fast vehicle check-in with on-site photos of existing damages and condition via smartphone or tablet.
2. **📷 Smart Vehicle Registration OCR Scanner** — automated extraction of VIN, license plates, make, model, and owner details without manual typing.
3. **🛠️ Real-Time Work Order Management** — live repair status tracking (*Intake -> Diagnostics -> Waiting for Parts -> In Progress -> Ready for Pickup*).
4. **📦 Parts Inventory & Barcode / QR System** — accurate tracking of stock arrivals, usage, work order reservations, and low-stock alerts.
5. **🔍 VIN-Based Service History** — full record of past repairs, replaced components, mechanic notes, and mileage logs for every vehicle.
6. **🔔 Automated Customer Reminders** — SMS or email notifications for routine maintenance, tire changes, and registration renewals.
7. **📱 Customer Live Status Portal** — clients receive a unique link to monitor repair progress and approve additional work with one click.
8. **📊 Financials, Estimates & Invoicing** — labor hour tracking, parts billing, PDF estimate generation, and workshop profitability insights.

### + Technology & Workshop Mobility

- **PWA (Progressive Web App)** — runs like a native app on phones, tablets, and workshop PCs without app store downloads.
- **Touch-Optimized Workshop UI** — designed for quick, intuitive use on the shop floor by mechanics and service advisors.
- **Bilingual (SR / EN)** — instant language switching across the entire system.
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture** — strict data isolation for every auto shop managed via a central SuperAdmin dashboard.
